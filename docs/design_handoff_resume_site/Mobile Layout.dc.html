Why this is different from desktop
1 · Identity card, not banner. Name, role and status in one compact block. When you scroll, it shrinks into a slim top bar so your name never leaves the screen.
2 · Tabs instead of one long scroll. Work · Experience · Skills · About. A recruiter jumps straight to what they care about, without scrolling past six project cards on a 6" screen.
3 · Actions in the thumb zone. CV, LinkedIn and Email are pinned at the bottom, always one tap away. That fixes Strata pushing contact into the footer.
4 · Lead with the number. Featured cards open with the result (79%, 100%), then the title. Swipeable, so 3 flagships take up one screen height.
5 · Details on demand. Long descriptions move into a bottom sheet, and the timeline expands on tap. The default view stays scannable.
6 · Same design language. Strata's dark #1f1815 header, #49bf9d accent and Source Sans. Built as a separate mobile layout at the <736px breakpoint, while desktop keeps the side panel.
Dates, metrics and the email address are placeholders.
