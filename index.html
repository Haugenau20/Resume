About

I'm a Platform Engineer at SAAB Danmark A/S, where I roll out AI tooling for developers: sandboxed coding agents and autonomous merge request reviews. I also work on Ansible, OS setup and a shared signing service, and I'm taking a course in Applied AI for Industry.

Before this, I worked in test automation at SAAB, and on embedded software, build systems and CI at LINAK A/S. I hold a B.Eng. in Electronics from the University of Southern Denmark and served as a Sergeant in the Danish Army.

When I'm not coding, you'll find me playing Dungeons & Dragons, exploring board games, or working on personal tech projects.
